# SCINTILLA

Creative direction agreed with the player: an eerie industrial city during the blackout, becoming beautiful and welcoming when power returns.

## Protagonist and objective

Scintilla is a small maintenance robot with a glowing eye and a visible battery. She wakes in an unpowered workshop and must reach the district generator. Her tool produces a short discharge to defend against malfunctioning drones. Initial integrity is three points. The battery does not continuously drain.

## First level

Workshop 07 introduces a safe starting area, jumping, drones and exposed cables, a maintenance station, a freight lift over a shaft, and the final approach to the generator. Optional energy fragments encourage exploration. Ordinary checkpoint respawns preserve collected energy; restarting reloads the level and clears it. Exhausting the selected difficulty's attempts also clears checkpoints and energy.

## Transformation

Before restoration: dark blues and grays, nearly unlit windows, industrial silhouettes, and isolated electrical signals. Afterwards: amber windows, readable structures, warm light, and regrown foliage. Restoration happens in the scene with an active camera. The camera pulls back to show the district before the rating popup and level selector.

## Prototype

Gameplay takes place on the XZ plane with an orthographic camera and 2.5D presentation. The first original Blender kit includes the maintenance robot, drone, generator, checkpoint, streetlight, crate, pipe, facade, platform, energy crystal, and electrodes. Beveled surfaces and petrol blue, brass, and cyan materials establish the initial visual direction. Decorative geometry remains separate from collision. Paper2D remains available for 2D elements.

Enhanced Input maps A/D or arrows, Space, J for attack, E for interaction, and U for pause, with gamepad equivalents. W faces the city and S faces the camera. Original placeholder audio is synthesized by the scripts. The campaign has expanded to eight districts, mechanical hazards, and three difficulty settings; see [campaign](campaign.md) and [difficulty](difficulty.md).
