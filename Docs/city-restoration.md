# Fallen cities and power restoration

All eight districts use an original Blender kit with three masonry facades, a separate roof, rubble, dry vines with cracks and rust streaks, a bare tree, foliage, and rolling terrain. Facades vary in height, width, and depth. Each district retains its palette and distinctive props.

Before the generator, structures lean, roofs sit low and out of alignment, windows are dark, trees are bare, and the ground is dim. Rubble and dry vegetation remain visible during the route. Existing decorative props also lean and darken. This geometry has no collision; platforms, enemies, and jumps retain their gameplay behavior.

## Victory sequence

1. The generator records the victory and saves unlocks. Scintilla stops; choosing a level remains blocked.
2. A glowing line crosses the district. Repairs propagate from the generator with distance-based delays: facades and roofs realign, rubble and dry vines retract, windows and props brighten, and tree canopies regrow.
3. The orthographic camera pulls back and moves to the district center. Its final width covers the whole city, including extended levels. The restored panorama remains visible before the menu.
4. At 7.5 seconds, the [mission rating](mission-rating.md) appears, based on time, damage taken, and energy collected.
5. **CONTINUE** opens the eight-map selector. Only unlocked levels are available. Repeated victories do not add unlocks.

Repairs animate transforms and colors of separate components rather than using a physical fracture simulation. Transitions use smooth curves. The camera stops following Scintilla during the sequence. Victory is saved immediately so it survives closing the session before the panorama ends.

## Sources and recovery

- Blender: `Assets/Models/City/Scintilla_CityKit.blend`, nine FBX files, and `manifest.json`.
- Unreal: `/Game/Platformer/Art/City`; assemblies generated by `BP_City` in all eight maps.
- `Scripts/create_city_models.py` creates and exports models in the connected Blender instance.
- `Scripts/import_city_assets.py` imports meshes and instances of existing rigid materials.
- `Scripts/apply_city_restoration.py` creates `_BeforeRestoration` recovery maps, updates only the city, and marks repairable props without changing routes.
- `Scripts/playtest_restoration.py` checks the complete sequence on eight maps using isolated saves.
- `Scripts/capture_restoration.py` captures four stages in PIE and restores the player's original save before restarting a normal game.

Maps are explicitly updated after compiling the native module. If an older campaign builder reconstructs a map, rerun `apply_city_restoration.py` to restore the city assembly and prop tags. Recovery copies are created locally and excluded from version control.

## Grounding on existing terrain

Buildings and trees follow the existing terrain mesh profile. Each facade foundation uses the lowest point under its width, with slight burial to avoid floating corners on slopes even in the leaning pose. The roof and attached vegetation receive the same offset. Each tree is rooted at local ground height; its canopy retains the trunk's origin during regrowth. Rubble also rests on the surface.

`Scripts/ground_city_scenery.py` updates only these assemblies in all eight maps, making `_BeforeGrounding` copies. It verifies that the terrain mesh and transform remain unchanged and the asset retains the same SHA-256. The profile extracted from the imported mesh is stored in the map, so packaged gameplay does not need CPU access to vertices. Native `BuildCity` applies the same grounding to newly built assemblies.

The shared terrain extends about 200 metres below the skyline so the restoration panorama does not reveal the background under the bank. Only the four bottom vertices were lowered; the uneven surface and scenery foundations stay in place. `Scripts/deepen_city_terrain.py` updates the Blender source and FBX, and `Scripts/import_deep_city_terrain.py` imports the mesh and checks the reveal framing in all eight districts. Source recovery copies are under `Saved/ScintillaBackup/BeforeDeepTerrain`; the Unreal recovery mesh is `SM_CityTerrain_BeforeDeepTerrain`.
